About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing short notes before you forget them. It keeps all your ideas in one place so you can find them again later.

How to use QuickNotes

  • Open the app and click the "New note" box.
  • Type your idea.
  • Click Add Note to save it to your list.
  • Check the "Your notes" list to see everything you have saved.

Features

  • Fast note creation
  • A simple, clean list of your notes
  • A running count of your notes
  • Works on phones and computers

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Start a new note
Enter Add the note you typed
Tab Move to the next field
